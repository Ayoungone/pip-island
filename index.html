How Pip decides what comes next

  • Only answers that are right on the first try raise a score. Quick, confident answers raise it a little more.
  • Every letter, word and math fact has its own score. A miss lowers it, so that item comes back sooner.
  • New items are added a few at a time. When he gets nearly everything right, new ones come faster.
  • The next level opens once he's about a third of the way through the level before. Mastered items still come back now and then for review.
  • The Check-Up skips him past levels he already knows. Retake it every 4–6 weeks, or any time things look too easy.
